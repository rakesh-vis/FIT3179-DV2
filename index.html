Data preparation. LGA estimates were extracted from the PHIDU workbook with Python and joined to the LGA boundaries by LGA code. Boundaries were simplified and converted to TopoJSON with mapshaper. Areas with no published estimate are shown in grey. The Melbourne dot map places one dot per 200 people at random inside each council.
